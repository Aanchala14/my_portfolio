# Portfolio content checklist

The site structure and initial research case studies are complete. To make every item fully verifiable, gather the following content:

## Projects

- Final project titles and one-sentence outcomes
- Repository, paper, poster or demo links
- Dates and collaborators, where public
- One approved image per project

## Certifications

- Title, issuer and completion date for each certificate
- Credential URL or a PDF/image for each certificate

## References

- Signed Cambridge, UZH and VJTI letters
- Confirmation from each referee that the letter may be displayed publicly
- Preferred public wording for the referee’s name and title

Never publish personal phone numbers, signatures or direct referee contact details without explicit permission.
