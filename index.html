Machine learning research · University of Bristol

Length generalisation in Transformer sequence models

Building a configuration-driven PyTorch pipeline on AWS to train Transformer baselines on controlled algorithmic tasks and evaluate extrapolation at sequence lengths up to 1,024.

  • Redesigned a trivial copy benchmark as delayed copy to isolate long-range memory
  • Observed exact-match accuracy fall from 100% at the trained length to 0.8% at the first unseen length in preliminary addition experiments
  • Comparing absolute, sinusoidal, NoPE, RoPE and ALiBi representations across tasks and random seeds
